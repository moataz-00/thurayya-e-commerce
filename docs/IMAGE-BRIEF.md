# Photography brief

Every image on the site is currently a labelled placeholder. The label **is** the brief —
it names the shot the slot is waiting for. This document collects them so photography can
be commissioned without reading the code.

---

## How the placeholder works

```tsx
<ShotSlot label={{ en: "chandelier, front elevation", ar: "ثريا، واجهة أمامية" }} tone={2} />
```

- `label` — the brief, in both languages.
- `tone` — 1 to 4, lightest to deepest placeholder ground, so a page still reads as a
  composition before the photographs exist.
- `dark` — for slots on onyx or emerald.
- `src` — **when you pass this, the placeholder is replaced by the image.** That is the
  only change needed per slot.

When real photography lands, the label must be rewritten as a genuine `alt` description.
"hero — chandelier front elevation on ivory, 1600×2000" is a production note, not something
a screen reader should read out. See [ACCESSIBILITY.md](ACCESSIBILITY.md).

---

## Per product — six shots, every time

Generated by `shotsFor()` in `src/lib/mock/products.ts`, so every one of the 31 products
has the same set:

| Slot        | Brief                                                          | Ratio | Notes                                              |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `hero`      | Front elevation on ivory                                       | 4:5   | 1600×2000. Unlit. The catalogue shot.              |
| `detail`    | Brass arm and cup junction, macro (furniture: joint and edge)   | 1:1   | Close enough to read the patina and the file marks |
| `lit`       | At night, warm 2700K (furniture: raking light across the top)   | 1:1   | The reason someone buys it                         |
| `video`     | 12s rotation, muted loop                                       | 4:5   | Silent, autoplay, no controls chrome               |
| `lifestyle` | The room the piece belongs in                                  | 4:5   | Per-product, from the seed's `shotAlt`             |
| `scale`     | Dimension overlay drawing                                      | 4:5   | Line drawing over the hero, not a photograph       |

The card uses `hero` at rest and `lifestyle` on hover — that cross-fade is the single most
visible piece of photography direction on the site, so the two must be shot to frame
together.

## Category banners

One wide banner and one 3:4 tile per category, in `src/lib/mock/taxonomy.ts`. Banners are
2400×760 and sit at 18% opacity behind onyx, so they need strong shape and very little
detail. Examples:

- Chandeliers — chandelier gallery wall
- Pendants — pendant cluster over island
- Wall lights — sconce on plaster wall
- Outdoor — terrace at dusk
- Consoles — travertine console, side light

---

## Collections

Per collection (`src/lib/mock/collections.ts`): one full-bleed hero at 2400×1300 and three
campaign frames — a wide room shot, a material macro, and one behind-the-scenes frame from
the atelier. The behind-the-scenes frame is not optional: it is what separates the
collection pages from a catalogue.

---

## Rooms

Per room (`src/lib/mock/rooms.ts`): one 2400×1200 hero, shot at the hour named in the room
copy, plus a 16:11 tile.

**The heroes carry hotspots.** Each room declares two or three `{ x, y, slug }` percentage
positions that must land on the actual products in frame. If the composition changes, the
percentages have to change with it — they are in the room data, not in CSS.

---

## Journal

Per article: one 16:10 hero, plus any in-article `shot` blocks named in the body. The
diagram briefs ("chandelier scale study", "three light layers annotated", "table width to
fixture diameter") are illustrations, not photographs.

## Direction

Pulled from the art direction the design set out:

- **Warm, never white.** Backgrounds are Moon Ivory `#F2EEE5`, not paper white.
- **2700K.** Every lit shot at the same colour temperature as the fixtures ship with.
- **One light source that reads.** Lit products may carry one soft warm glow; the page adds
  a subtle animated version behind hero imagery, so the photograph should not fight it.
- **Space.** Photography is given the room a showroom would give an object. Crops are
  generous; the object does not fill the frame.
- **No people in product shots.** Lifestyle frames may include a room in use; the catalogue
  hero is the object alone.
- **Never mirrored.** Photography is never flipped for RTL.

## Wiring images in

1. Put the files under `public/` (or a CDN once one exists).
2. Set `src` on the `Shot` records in the mock layer — the type already has the field.
3. Rewrite each `label` as a real alt description.
4. Swap the `<img>` inside `ShotSlot` for `next/image` and add the host to
   `images.remotePatterns` in `next.config.ts` if the files are remote.

That is four steps for the whole site, because every image goes through one component.
